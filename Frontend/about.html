What is EcoSphere?

EcoSphere is a smart waste management platform designed to make reporting and resolving waste problems easier.

Users can report waste by uploading an image and providing its location. Artificial intelligence analyzes the image and identifies the type of waste.

Once a report is submitted, it can be assigned to a worker and tracked until the issue is resolved.
